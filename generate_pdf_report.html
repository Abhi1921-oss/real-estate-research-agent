1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

The Multi-City Real Estate Daily Research Multi-Agent System is an end-to-end intelligence platform engineered specifically for independent property brokers, channel partner agencies, and real estate consultants in high-velocity Indian urban markets.

In fast-moving real estate ecosystems such as Pune and Lucknow, brokers lose 1 to 2 hours every morning sifting through fragmented RERA filings, developer brochures, bank interest announcements, and government circle rate circulars. This manual overhead creates severe information delays and leads to lost client conversions.

Core Mission: Automate the entire market research pipeline through three concurrent sub-agents and deliver a verified, jargon-free, 2-minute actionable morning brief with pre-formatted WhatsApp client pitch scripts before the business day begins.

Key Value Drivers for Real Estate Brokers:

  • Zero Research Overhead: Parallel sub-agents crawl and verify pricing, launches, and policy in under 2 seconds.
  • Client Sales Acceleration: Generates ready-to-speak 20-second telephone pitch scripts and emoji-formatted WhatsApp broadcast messages.
  • Legal Safety & Trust: Flags mandatory RERA compliance (such as MahaRERA's 10% advance payment cap and UP RERA model agreements).
  • Financial Closeness: Instant buyer loan EMI calculator benchmarked to live repo rate floors (7.10% – 7.25% p.a.).

2. Multi-Agent System Architecture

Rather than relying on a slow, monolithic script, the system deploys a decoupled, parallel multi-agent architecture. Sub-Agents 1 and 2 operate simultaneously across different data domains, and their verified outputs are fed into Sub-Agent 3 for executive synthesis.

Sub-Agent 01: Prices & Launches Tracker

Primary Function: Tracks micro-market transaction bands, rental yield percentages, and verified RERA launches.

Coverage: Kharadi, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner (Pune) | Gomti Nagar, Shaheed Path, Sushant Golf City, Ayodhya Rd (Lucknow).

Output: ₹/sq.ft averages, observed price ranges, developer launch radar.

Sub-Agent 02: Policy & Banking Monitor

Primary Function: Crawls macro monetary policy, state circle rates, and regulatory statutory updates.

Coverage: RBI Repo Rate (5.25%), bank home loan floors, UP 1% female stamp duty rebate, MahaRERA Section 13 rules.

Output: Financing benchmarks, compliance checklists, buyer alerts.

Sub-Agent 03: Synthesis & Pitch Engine

Primary Function: Consolidates data streams, removes unverifiable marketing noise, attaches confidence tags, and produces human-friendly sales assets.

Assets Generated: 1-page printable PDF brief, 20-second telephone script, WhatsApp broadcast with emojis, and structured JSON feeds.

4. Regulatory & Financing Watchdog

Maharashtra (MahaRERA & PMC/PCMC)

  • Strict 10% Advance Cap: Section 13 mandates that builders cannot demand >10% advance without registered Agreement for Sale.
  • Mandatory QR Codes: Every advertisement, brochure, and website must link to the MahaRERA project progress filing.
  • Ready Reckoner Stability: State government held ASR rates steady, avoiding unexpected stamp duty jumps.

Uttar Pradesh (UP RERA & LDA)

  • 1% Female Stamp Duty Rebate: Stamp duty reduced to 6% (from 7%) when registered in a woman's name—saving clients ₹60k–₹1L.
  • Model Agreement Compliance: Builder delay compensation legally pinned to SBI MCLR + 1%.
  • LDA Approval Vigilance: Warning against unauthorized farm plot subdivisions along Kisan Path peripheries.
